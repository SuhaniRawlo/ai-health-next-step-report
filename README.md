# From Health Information to the Next Right Step

Project report for the AI Careers for Women – LinkedIn Fellowship.

## About
Young women have access to more health information than ever, but often
struggle to decide what to do with it. This project explores the gap between
finding health information and knowing the next right step, focusing on
college students in India.

## Category
Healthcare AI

## Tools
Google Gemini, Power BI, Otter.ai

## Contents
- Problem statement and objectives
- Review of existing solutions and published evidence
- Methodology, project phases and data analysis plan
- Progress report, action plan and conclusion
